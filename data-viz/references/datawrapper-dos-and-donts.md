- **Date:** 2017–2026 (living category)

This is the series Saloni points to as further reading. Posts already given their own files stay there (text, colorblind, chart types, questions, tables, dual-axis). Below is the rest of the **form-choice** core, distilled from those posts.

## Lines ([line-charts](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog/line-charts))

Time, not categories (those are bars). Lines for quantities that persist (population); columns when each period fills from zero (sales) or you have few aligned points. Many points or irregular intervals → line. If the *sum* of series is the story, area. Include zero when the data already live near it; include 100% when shares sit near the ceiling. Smooth curves hide and distort; if you curve, use the less theatrical interpolation.

## Pies ([pie-charts](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog/pie-charts))

One total, few slices, ideally near quarters. Not for close comparisons, not for two values (just write the number), not for comparing two wholes (stacked bars). Label small slices outside; group “other.” They waste width — often a sidebar.

## Choropleths ([choroplethmaps](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog/choroplethmaps))

The geographic *pattern*, not a subtle ranking. Color is a poor magnitude scale; small regions disappear. If the point is “how much more is A than B,” use bars, a table, or prose. Sequential vs diverging with a real midpoint; colorblind-safe. Discrete classes = fast bins, lost neighbors; continuous = neighbor comparison, slower bins. Legend with even ticks and the center on a diverging scale.
